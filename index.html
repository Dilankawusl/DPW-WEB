About Me

Dilanka Wijesena

I am Dilanka Wijesena, a Lecturer and researcher passionate about transforming innovative ideas into intelligent real-world solutions. My interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Embedded Systems, and IoT, with a strong focus on integrating modern AI technologies with practical engineering applications to create impactful and future-oriented systems.

Beyond research and development, I am actively involved in academic teaching, student mentoring, and research supervision, helping students build both technical knowledge and problem-solving skills. I enjoy collaborating on innovative projects, exploring emerging technologies, and contributing to the growth of technology-driven education and research.

Driven by curiosity and continuous learning, I strive to bridge the gap between theory and practical innovation while developing solutions that can make a meaningful impact on society.
